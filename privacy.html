Privacy Policy

Crown International Education (Pvt.) Limited. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

This policy explains how Crown International Education (Pvt.) Limited ("Crown International", "we", "us") handles personal information when you contact us on WhatsApp, through our website, or in person, including when you chat with our automated assistant.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, WhatsApp phone number and preferred language.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, and our replies.
  • Study and visa enquiry details you choose to share: for example your education, grades, language test scores, budget, countries and courses of interest, work experience, and documents or CV information you send.
  • Appointment details: times you book for a call with a counselor, and the outcome of the call.

2. How we use it

  • To answer your questions about our services, universities and programmes.
  • To check, at a basic level, whether an offer or programme may suit your profile, and to pass your enquiry to a suitable counselor.
  • To book, change or cancel calls and to follow up on your enquiry.
  • To alert our staff about new enquiries and cases that need attention.
  • To protect our service from spam and abuse, and to improve how we help students.

3. Automated assistant

Some replies on WhatsApp are written by an automated AI assistant. It gives general information only. It does not make final decisions about admissions or visas. A member of our team can take over your conversation at any time, and you can ask to speak to a person.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate: Meta / WhatsApp (to deliver messages), our hosting and database provider, our workflow-automation provider, and our AI service provider. They may process your information only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and our related legal and business obligations. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see section 7).

6. Security

Access to your information is limited to our authorised staff, with sign-in protection. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop receiving messages from us, by emailing info@cecpak.com. See also our Data Deletion page.

8. Children

Our services are for people aged 18 and over, or for parents and guardians acting for a student. We do not knowingly collect information from children without a parent or guardian involved.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
