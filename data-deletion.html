Data Deletion Instructions

Crown International Education (Pvt.) Limited. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you, including your WhatsApp conversation, profile details and appointment records.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@cecpak.com with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used to contact us (with country code) and your name, so we can find your records.

What happens next

  • We may ask you to confirm that the request comes from you.
  • We will delete or anonymise your information within 30 days, and confirm to you when done.
  • We may keep limited records where the law requires it, or to settle a dispute, and will tell you if so.

See our Privacy Policy for more about how we handle information.
